About Tawsila

Tawsila is a community carpooling app that helps people share rides. It is run on a non-commercial basis. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you use the app or message our WhatsApp number, why we handle it, and the choices you have. In this policy, "Tawsila", "we" and "us" mean the people who run the app.

Information we collect

Account details. Your mobile phone number, your name and, if you add one, a profile photo.

Ride details. Pickup and drop-off points, routes, dates and times, number of seats, ride requests and your ride history.

Location. If you allow location access, we use your device location to show nearby rides and to share a pickup point with the people on your ride. You can turn location off in your device settings.

Messages. If you message our WhatsApp number or contact support, we keep the messages so we can reply and fix problems.

Technical data. Device type, app version and basic logs that help us find and fix errors.

WhatsApp and sign-in codes

To sign you in, we send a one-time code to your phone number on WhatsApp. We use the WhatsApp Business Platform, which is provided by Meta. The message contains only the code.

  • We keep a record that a code was sent and whether it was delivered. We do not store the code itself.
  • Meta processes your phone number and message delivery data under its own privacy policy.
  • We only message you on WhatsApp for sign-in codes and replies to messages you send us.

How we use information

  • To create your account and verify your phone number.
  • To show, request and arrange rides, and to let riders and drivers contact each other.
  • To keep the community safe, prevent misuse and investigate reports.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To maintain and improve the app.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not build marketing profiles.

Who we share it with

Other people on your ride. Riders and drivers on the same trip can see your name and the pickup details needed to meet.

Service providers. We use Supabase to host our database and Meta (WhatsApp) to send codes and messages. They handle data only to provide these services to us.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. Ride history and message logs are kept for as long as they are needed for safety, support and legal reasons, then deleted or anonymised. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal information, except what we must keep by law.

Security

We protect your data with access controls, encrypted connections and restricted database access. No system is completely secure, so please keep your phone and WhatsApp account protected and never share your sign-in code.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal information.
  • Turn off location access in your device settings at any time.
  • Stop WhatsApp messages by not using phone sign-in or by telling us to stop.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will reply within a reasonable time. These rights are in line with applicable data protection law, including Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020).

How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete your Tawsila account and the personal information linked to it at any time.

  1. Send an email to privacy@designmasr.com with the subject "Delete my Tawsila data".
  2. Write the mobile phone number you use with Tawsila, so we can find your account.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you, using the same WhatsApp number or email.

We will delete your account, profile, ride history and message logs within 30 days. We may keep a small amount of information if the law requires it, for example to resolve a safety report, and we delete it when that need ends. We will confirm by email when the deletion is complete.

Age limit

Tawsila is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Where data is stored

Our hosting provider may store data on servers outside Egypt. When this happens, we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact us

For questions, data requests or account deletion, email privacy@designmasr.com.
